Research question. How reliably do language models interpret emotions, context, and implicit meaning in literary excerpts, and are those interpretations grounded in textual evidence?

Research limitations and future vision

What this pilot is

  • A four-excerpt qualitative pilot, not a validated benchmark.
  • The interface shows pre-generated outputs. No live AI inference happens here.
  • Differences between models are preliminary and do not establish that any model is generally better.
  • Model-stated confidence is self-report, not evidence that an interpretation is correct.

Proposed work

  • The immersive narrative reader is a future vision, not a completed product.
  • A transcription-inspired approach to literary interpretation is proposed work, not a reproduced or validated method.
  • LitEval-15 is described as 15 excerpts from five public-domain works. This workbook set contains four of them.

Data notes and flags

The three workbooks were joined automatically on excerpt ID and model name. Anything that could not be matched with confidence, or that required interpretation, is listed here.
